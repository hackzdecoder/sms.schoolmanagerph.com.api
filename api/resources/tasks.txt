1.) SchoolMANAGER ACCOUNTS PAGE
STATISTICAL APPLETS
1. Previous Account Balance - clickable
2. Current Enrollment Fee Balance - clickable
3. Total Current Installment Balance - clickable
4. Other Fees Account Balance - clickable
5. Total Account Balance - clickable
6. Prepaid Account Available Balance - clickable
7. Current Installment Account Dues - clickable
8. Current Overdue Installment Accounts - clickable

Notes:
Previous Account Balance applet displays the Summation (Total) of all the Unpaid Balance/s in the Previous School
Year/s.
Current Enrollment Fee Balance applet displays the Unpaid Balance in the Current School Year’s Enrollment Fees.
Total Current Installment Balance applet displays the Summation (Total) of all the Unpaid Monthly Balance/s in the
Current School Year (12 Months from June to May)
Other Fees Account Balance applet displays the Summation (Total) of all the Unpaid Balance/s on Other Fees in the
system.
Total Account Balance applet displays the Sum of the Student’s Previous Account Balance, Current Enrollment Balance,
Total Current Installment Balance, and Total Other Fees Account Balances.
Prepaid Account Available Balance applet displays the current Available Balance on the Student’s Prepaid Account.
Current Installment Account Dues applet displays the current amount dues and its due date (for the Current School
Year) relative to the date of the system and the installment due dates in the database.
Current Overdue Installment Accounts applet displays the amount of overdue monthly installments (for the Current
School Year) relative to the date of the system and the installment due dates in the database. Records will be based on
the monthly dues that has an overdue status in the monthly_account_balance table.
